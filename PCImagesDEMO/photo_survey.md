# 50-Address KartaView Coverage Survey — Halifax

Generated by running `fetch_kartaview_images()` + `filter_facing_images()` from
[halifax_lod_demo.py](halifax_lod_demo.py) against 50 real, randomly-sampled
addressed buildings in the Halifax peninsula (pulled from OSM/Overpass, not
invented). For each address with at least one image whose camera bearing
plausibly faces the building, the closest such photo was downloaded and
personally reviewed below. Photos live in [photos/](photos/), named by index.

**Coverage: 24 of 50 addresses (48%) had a usable, downloaded facing photo.**
2 more addresses had a facing image but its file was undownloadable (a
KartaView storage node, `storage7.openstreetcam.org`, was serving 502s for
every 2018-vintage photo at the time of this run — a live infrastructure
outage, not a code bug). The remaining 24 had no image within the search
radius whose heading pointed at the building — expected for a crowdsourced
dataset in residential side streets.

Ratings are my own 1–10 read on how usable each photo is for the pipeline's
opening-detection step specifically: is the actual target facade in frame,
sharp, well-lit, and unobstructed by traffic/foliage/signage/dashboard.
Average across the 24 rated photos: **4.5/10** — a bimodal set, in practice:
a strong cluster of real, close, sharp facade shots on Barrington/Hollis
Street, and a weak cluster of dashcam-style shots where the "photo" is mostly
windshield, dashboard, and a building three blocks away.

## Takeaways for the pipeline

- **Downtown corridors win.** Every 6+/10 photo is on Barrington, Hollis, or
  Oxford Street — dense, frequently-driven downtown streets with heavy
  KartaView coverage. Coverage on residential side streets and the peninsula
  edges is close to zero.
- **The dashcam-mount images are the main quality drag.** A large chunk of
  the low scores aren't bad photos of the right thing — they're clear photos
  of a dashboard, with the actual building three lanes away. `filter_facing_images()`
  correctly identifies these as "facing" (the bearing math is right), but
  bearing alone can't tell a close, square-on facade shot from a windshield
  shot of a building half a block down the road. A distance-weighted or
  frame-fraction check (how much of the image plausibly shows building vs.
  road/sky/dashboard) would filter a lot of this out before it reaches
  opening detection.
- **The `storage7.openstreetcam.org` 502s were transient infrastructure,**
  not a pipeline bug — 2 addresses lost their only good facing image to it.
  Worth a retry-later pass if this survey is rerun.
